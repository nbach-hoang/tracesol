## Rules, eligibility, and judging

Source: [official rules](https://launchpad-hacks.devpost.com/rules).

- Entrants must be eligible students above the legal age of majority in their country of residence and meet the displayed geographic restrictions. Age and residence eligibility have not been verified here. Solo and team entries are permitted; organizers and judges cannot compete.
- Submit one entry per project. Disclose event-period contributions and pre-existing foundations. AI assistance and third-party components are allowed with proper credit and lawful use.
- Required judging links must remain accessible through 25 November, Eastern Time. Local file paths and localhost URLs are not public judging links.
- Four equal criteria: originality, usefulness/impact, implementation/functionality, and design/presentation. Ties prioritize usefulness, then implementation, then judge comparison.
- No private information, secrets, malicious content, plagiarism, harassment, or interference.
