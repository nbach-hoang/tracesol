Column guide & supported units

Required: record_id, compound, value, unit. Matching conditions also need form, solvent, ph, temperature_c, method, measurement_kind. Add mw_g_mol for mass units and source, evidence, source_locator for traceability.

Units: mol/L, M, mmol/L, mM, uM/µM, umol/L, nM, nmol/L, g/L, mg/L, ug/L, mg/mL, ug/mL, logS. logS means log10(S / [1 mol/L]). ppm and molality are deliberately unsupported.
